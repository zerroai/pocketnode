# Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | Nunito | 800 | Outlined in the logo files. Never retype it |
| Large titles and brand headings | Nunito | 800 | Proposed. The app uses Inter bold today |
| App text | Inter (in the app now) | 400 to 700 | 16 sp body, 24 line height |
| Alternative app text | Nunito | 400 to 700 | If the owner prefers a rounder app. Try on the phone first |

`fonts/Nunito-Variable.ttf` is the variable font (weights 200 to 1000). Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1, `fonts/OFL-Nunito.txt`. The OFL allows use and bundling in a paid, closed app. Do not sell the font on its own.

The app ships static subsets of Inter at about 74 KB each. If Nunito is adopted, subset it the same way (Latin plus the Naira sign) and keep it to three weights.

Scale (sp): large title 34, title 22, headline 16 semibold, body 16, callout 15, subhead 14, footnote 12.5. Unchanged from `docs/design/ui-guidelines.md`.
